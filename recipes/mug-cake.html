Microwave Mug Cake

How to make the most delicious homemade mug cake ever!!

A mug cake is such an easy and satisfying dessert or little snack to make whenever you are craving something sweet!!

Ingredients:

  • Flour
  • Baking powder
  • Powdered sugar
  • Cocoa powder
  • Vanilla extract
  • Frosting
  • Optional sprinkes

Steps:

  1. Add all your dry ingredients to the mug and mix with a fork
  2. mix your wet ingredients seperately until homogenous
  3. Add your wet ingredients into the mug with the dry ingredients and whisk until no lumps remain
  4. Microwave for 1 minute on 800w
  5. Add frosting and optional sprinkles
  6. Enjoy!
